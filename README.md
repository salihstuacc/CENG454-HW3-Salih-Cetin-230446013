##  Gameplay & Controls 

The main objective is to defend the central energy core against escalating waves of hostile robots. 

* **Mouse Left Click :** Place defensive turrets on the ground to protect the tower.
* **Key 1 :** Apply a permanent **Damage Upgrade** to the turrets, dynamically wrapping the weapon mechanics using the Decorator pattern.
* **Key 2 :** Apply a permanent **Attack Speed Upgrade** (reduces cooldown) to increase the firing rate of the turrets.
